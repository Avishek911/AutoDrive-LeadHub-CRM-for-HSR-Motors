OVERVIEW
HSR Motors is a multi-brand car dealership advertising across Facebook Ads, Google Search, Twitter/X, Website forms, and Offline Expo Events.

PROBLEM STATEMENT:
Currently, HSR Motors relies on manual spreadsheets to track lead status (New, Contacted, Test Drive, Won, Lost). Spreadsheets suffer from major operational bottlenecks:
1. No Real-time Collaboration: Sales reps overwrite data, leading to double-calls or unassigned leads.
2. High Lead Decay & SLA Breaches: Average response time exceeds 4 hours due to manual routing.
3. Zero Source Attribution: Business managers cannot trace which ad campaigns generate high-intent buyers versus tire-kickers.
4. Scattered Test Drive Management: Lack of visibility into home test drive slots vs showroom walk-ins.

SOLUTION APPROACH:
AutoDrive LeadHub CRM is a purpose-built desktop business application designed for two core roles:
• Sales Team: High-speed, 360-degree lead detail view, SLA countdowns, 1-click test drive scheduling, and Gemini AI call/WhatsApp script generators.
• Business Manager: Executive analytical dashboard with channel ROI attribution, real-time conversion funnels, sales rep leaderboards, and automated lead routing rules.

---

FEATURES
1. Multi-Channel Centralized Lead Inbox: Real-time lead listing with multi-dimensional filtering (Source, Status, Rep, Vehicle, Budget) and SLA timers.
2. 360° Lead Customer Details & Activity Timeline: Comprehensive customer dossier tracking trade-in details, financing status, call logs, and SMS history.
3. Integrated Test Drive Booking Engine: Schedule Home Test Drives or Showroom Visits with calendar slot conflict checks and rep assignment.
4. Interactive Lead Management Kanban Pipeline: Drag-and-drop workflow status transitions with visual SLA breach warnings (< 2 hour limit).
5. Automated Lead Distribution Engine: Configurable rule builder routing leads automatically based on budget thresholds and channel sources.
6. Business Analytics & Lead Source Attribution: Interactive charts tracking lead conversion rates, campaign CAC/ROI, vehicle demand, and sales rep leaderboards.
7. Gemini AI Sales Copilot: Integrated AI generator producing tailored call scripts, WhatsApp proposals, and executive lead risk assessments.

WIREFRAME / MOCKUP / PROTOTYPE
- Screen 1: Lead Listing (Search, Multi-Filter, SLA Timers, Bulk Reassignment)
- Screen 2: Lead Details (360° Customer Profile, Test Drive Booking Engine, Activity Log, Gemini AI Copilot)
- Screen 3: Lead Management (Interactive Kanban Pipeline & Auto-Routing Rules Engine)
- Screen 4: Business Dashboard (Source Attribution, Funnel Dropoff, Model Demand Heatmap, Rep Leaderboard)

USER FLOW
1. Lead Ingestion: Prospect submits a query on Facebook/Google/Website or registers at an Offline Event.
2. Smart Auto-Routing: LeadHub's Rule Engine evaluates budget (> ₹20L) and channel, assigning the lead instantly to the optimal Sales Rep (e.g., Priya Nair).
3. SLA Notification & 360° Inspection: Sales Rep receives an SLA timer alert (30 mins max response). Opens Screen 2 (Lead Details) to review customer trade-in details and budget.
4. AI-Assisted Outreach: Sales Rep clicks "Gemini AI Script Generator" to generate a custom 45-second phone script and schedules a Home Test Drive for Sunday.
5. Kanban & Pipeline Progression: Sales Rep updates status to "Test Drive Scheduled" on Screen 3 (Kanban Board).
6. Deal Closure & Manager Oversight: Upon booking, status shifts to "Won". The Business Manager reviews campaign ROI, rep conversion benchmarks, and model demand trends on Screen 4 (Dashboard).
